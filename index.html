PROJECT 01 · C++ / COMPUTER ARCHITECTURE

Cache Coherence Simulator

↗

A configurable simulator for studying cache hierarchy behavior and directory-based MESI coherence in multi-core systems.

What it models

Multi-core cache hierarchies with inclusive, exclusive and NINE inclusion policies, alongside directory-based MESI protocol behavior.

What I implemented

LRU and Belady's optimal replacement policies, plus miss classification into cold, conflict and capacity misses using a fully associative reference model.

Why it matters

The simulator makes memory-system behavior observable and provides a way to compare how cache organization and replacement decisions affect workloads.

03 / RESEARCH

Multi-robot reactive motion planning

MS by Research · Computer Science & Engineering · IIT Kanpur · 2020–2023

DRDO-SPONSORED RESEARCH · CAIR, BENGALURU

Planning missions for heterogeneous robots when the world does not stay still.

Read ICCPS 2024 paper ↗ Research videos ↗ Supervisor · Prof. Indranil Saha ↗

Problem

Given complex Linear Temporal Logic (LTL) mission specifications and a team of heterogeneous robots, generate plans at runtime that satisfy the mission while adapting to changes in the environment. The planning problem also considers response time and the path length travelled by each robot.

Environmental constraints included dynamic obstacles, robot battery limitations and intruder or human-presence events.

Approach

Developed a runtime framework based on a Sense–Plan–Act paradigm. The framework incorporates changes in the environment and generates or updates robot plans while satisfying LTL mission constraints.

The simulation setup used ROS and Gazebo with a heterogeneous team of three Husky unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) and four micro aerial vehicles (MAVs).

Robotics integration

  • Integrated ZED stereo camera, GPS and 360° LiDAR sensors with NVIDIA Jetson and Intel NUC compute modules on three 0x Delta UGV platforms using ROS.
  • Fused odometry, IMU and GPS measurements using Kalman filters and variants to estimate continuous robot pose indoors and outdoors.
  • Tested PX4 and ArduPilot flight-stack modes on assembled MAV platforms.
  • Configured ROS Navigation Stack components for dynamic point-to-point navigation, including SLAM, A* global planning and the Timed Elastic Band local planner.
  • Demonstrated trajectory generation for point-to-point MAV navigation.

Demonstrations & publication

The work was demonstrated on physical robotic platforms at IIT Kanpur and CAIR-DRDO. The research was published at the ACM/IEEE International Conference on Cyber-Physical Systems (ICCPS 2024).

R. Singh and I. Saha, “An Online Planning Framework for Multi-Robot Systems with LTL Specification,” ACM/IEEE 15th International Conference on Cyber-Physical Systems (ICCPS), Hong Kong, 2024, pp. 180–191.
